How your study plan works

New words need three consecutive successful recalls, ending with Easy. Forgetting resets the streak. Words repeat with other cards between them; when only one remains, a 30-second gap replaces the intervening card. The whole batch moves into revision together.

Revision uses FSRS at a 90% target retention. Early reviews also update the schedule. Initial learning and revision attempts are counted separately. Keyboard: Space to reveal; 1, 2, 3 to rate; U to undo.
